# Popcase

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers view photographs in published EmDash content. Photographers want to share the camera, lens, and exposure information recorded in those photographs. Site administrators control viewer behavior.

## Product Purpose

Popcase enhances existing content with an image and video viewer without requiring authors to edit or resave it. Its photo information feature makes seven photography fields available on request while preserving the viewing experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The agreed EXIF scope and interactions are recorded in `.scratch/popcase-exif/spec.md`. Photo information belongs to the actual viewer image. Public and admin labels are English. Keep responsive previews, captions, zoom, mixed-media navigation, and ordinary image-link fallbacks. Viewer code loads on first interaction; EXIF reads begin only when the information panel is expanded.

## Brand Commitments

Retain Popcase's existing dark, partially transparent viewer backdrop in both host themes. Use Lucide for the new photography interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The viewer supports keyboard and touch use, contained focus, focus restoration, and an information panel with explicit labels. Icons supplement text. Failures do not prevent viewing or dismissal.
